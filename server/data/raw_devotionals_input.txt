# INNER PEACE — 1–100 BIBLE DEVOTIONALS

### 1. Peace Begins With God

**Bible Verse:** “Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.”
**Scripture Reference:** Isaiah 26:3

**Reflection:** Inner peace begins when our minds remain focused on God. Trusting Him gives our hearts stability even when life is uncertain.

**Prayer:** Lord, keep my mind stayed on You and fill me with perfect peace. Amen.

### 2. A Quiet Soul

**Bible Verse:** “My soul, wait thou only upon God; for my expectation is from him.”
**Scripture Reference:** Psalm 62:5

**Reflection:** Our hearts become restless when we place our expectations in people or circumstances. True peace comes from waiting on God.

**Prayer:** Father, teach my soul to wait quietly upon You. Amen.

### 3. God Is My Refuge

**Bible Verse:** “God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.”
**Scripture Reference:** Psalm 46:1

**Reflection:** When life becomes overwhelming, God remains a safe place for the heart. Run to Him instead of allowing trouble to control your thoughts.

**Prayer:** Lord, be my refuge whenever my heart feels troubled. Amen.

### 4. Be Still Before God

**Bible Verse:** “Be still, and know that I am God.”
**Scripture Reference:** Psalm 46:10

**Reflection:** Inner peace sometimes requires us to stop trying to control everything and remember that God is still in control.

**Prayer:** Lord, quiet my heart and help me remember that You are God. Amen.

### 5. Peace Through Prayer

**Bible Verse:** “Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God.”
**Scripture Reference:** Philippians 4:6

**Reflection:** Prayer gives our worries somewhere to go. Instead of carrying every concern inside, give it to God.

**Prayer:** Father, help me bring every concern to You in prayer. Amen.

### 6. Guarded by Peace

**Bible Verse:** “And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.”
**Scripture Reference:** Philippians 4:7

**Reflection:** God's peace can protect our hearts and minds even when we do not understand what is happening.

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, let Your peace guard my heart and mind today. Amen.

### 7. The Peace of Christ

**Bible Verse:** “Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you.”
**Scripture Reference:** John 14:27

**Reflection:** Jesus offers a peace that circumstances cannot manufacture and circumstances cannot easily remove.

**Prayer:** Jesus, fill my heart with the peace that only You can give. Amen.

### 8. Do Not Let Your Heart Be Troubled
